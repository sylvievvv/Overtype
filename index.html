    Help

    How to work with Overtyp.

    Adding files

    Drag files into the "Add documents" box or click Choose files. This works with PDF (including scans), photos, Word, Excel, CSV and emails. You can add several files at once.

    Adding an email

    Copy and paste: open the email, press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C, then press Ctrl+V in Overtyp. Attachments are not included this way.

    As a file, so attachments are read too:

    • Outlook on your computer: drag the email to your desktop, then drag that file into Overtyp.
    • New Outlook or Outlook on the web: open the email, click … and choose Download.
    • Gmail: open the email, click ⋮ and choose Download message.
    • Apple Mail: drag the email to your desktop.

    Reviewing

    Contacts that don't look quite right are listed under Needs review. Click an underlined value or the row. Fix the field and click Approve; you go straight to the next contact.

    Exporting

    Click Export to Excel and then Save. The file goes to your Downloads folder. Contacts that haven't been reviewed yet are on a separate "Needs review" sheet.

    My lists

    Every run is saved automatically under My lists in the menu, named after the first file. Click the name at the top to rename it. Open a list again later to add new files or pasted text to it; duplicate contacts are merged. + New starts an empty list.

    Adding to an existing list

    Choose your own file under Existing Excel list in the menu. When you export, you get that list back with only the new contacts added at the bottom.

    Privacy and data processing

    What happens to your files and contact details.

    • Files are read in your own browser. The original files are not uploaded or stored.
    • To recognise contact details, the text of the document (for scans, the page image) is processed once by Claude, through your own Claude account.
    • The lists you create (the contact details found) are saved in your own Claude account so you can add to them later. Only you can see your lists; colleagues and the maker of Overtyp cannot.
    • Delete list permanently erases a list and all contact details in it.
    • The Excel export is saved on your own computer. Only process data your organisation has a lawful basis for (GDPR).
